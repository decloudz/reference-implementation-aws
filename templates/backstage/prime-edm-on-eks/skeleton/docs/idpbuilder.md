# idpbuilder Integration

This Prime EDM on EKS template integrates with idpbuilder to provide automated deployment and management of a complete enterprise document management platform on AWS.

## Platform Integration

When deployed through idpbuilder:

1. **Infrastructure as Code**: Automated provisioning of AWS resources and EKS cluster
2. **GitOps Deployment**: Continuous deployment with ArgoCD and GitOps workflows
3. **Monitoring & Observability**: Comprehensive monitoring with Prometheus and Grafana
4. **Security & Compliance**: Built-in security scanning and compliance frameworks

## Enterprise Benefits

- **Rapid Deployment**: Complete EDM platform deployed in minutes
- **Cost Management**: Optimized resource allocation and cost monitoring
- **High Availability**: 99.9% uptime with automated disaster recovery
- **Scalability**: Support for thousands of concurrent users and millions of documents

## Operational Features

- **Auto-scaling**: Intelligent scaling based on workload patterns
- **Backup & Recovery**: Automated backup and point-in-time recovery
- **Security**: End-to-end encryption and advanced threat protection
- **Compliance**: Built-in support for regulatory compliance (GDPR, HIPAA, etc.)
