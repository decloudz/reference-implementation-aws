# idpbuilder Integration

This LOI service database template integrates with idpbuilder to provide comprehensive document and workflow management for Letter of Intent processes.

## Platform Integration

When deployed through idpbuilder:

1. **Secure Storage**: Enterprise-grade security for sensitive documents
2. **Workflow Automation**: Automated approval processes and notifications
3. **Compliance**: Built-in compliance tracking and audit capabilities
4. **Integration**: Seamless integration with business applications

## Business Benefits

- **Efficiency**: Streamlined LOI creation and approval processes
- **Compliance**: Automated compliance checking and reporting
- **Transparency**: Complete visibility into document status and workflows
- **Security**: Secure document storage and access controls
